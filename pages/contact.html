Before you write

Questions we get most

What happens after I send the form?

We read it, check whether we are a sensible fit, and reply with a time for a short call, usually within one working day. If your question is answerable by email, we answer it there instead of booking a meeting for the sake of it.

Is the first consultation really free?

The introductory conversation is free. If you want us to review your existing records before quoting, we will tell you upfront whether that review is chargeable and how much.

Can I get a BOSS demo without taking consulting?

Yes. BOSS is available as software only. Choose “BOSS Demo” in the form and we will walk you through the modules you need and share pricing for that package.

Do you work with businesses outside Kathmandu Valley?

Yes. Work is largely remote: documents, BOSS access and video calls, with in-person meetings arranged when they are genuinely useful.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your current records, any recent filings and a 30-minute conversation with whoever handles the accounts today. We send a checklist so nothing is missed.
