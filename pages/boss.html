Our product

BOSS, one platform for the financial and operational side of your business

Business Operation System Software, built by Bizmate. Accounts, payroll, attendance, CRM, tasks and hiring in one login, with our Chartered Accountants available to read the numbers with you.

Why it exists

Most business software ends at recording.

A ledger that balances tells you what happened. It does not tell you whether margin is slipping, whether a payroll change will strain cash next month, or whether the VAT return matches what the sales team actually invoiced.

BOSS was built inside a consulting practice, so it collects the records our team already needed to review. That is the difference: the software and the advice come from the same place.

  • Up-to-date information on accounts, cash, people and operations
  • Records maintained in a structured, audit-ready way
  • Dashboards that turn day-to-day entries into decisions
