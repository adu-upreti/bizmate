Who we are

A consulting practice built on correct books and honest advice

Bizmate Consulting Service is an accounting, taxation and financial consulting firm led by Chartered Accountants. We help business owners keep their numbers right, their compliance in order and their decisions well-informed.

Our approach

We start where the money actually is.

Most businesses come to us with a feeling rather than a figure. They sense something is off with margin, cash or compliance, but nobody can point at the line. Our first job is to make the records true.

From there, everything is easier: tax filings stop being a guess, payroll stops surprising you, and a bank or investor conversation becomes a set of statements instead of a story.

We also build our own software, BOSS. It means the system your team uses is the one our accountants review: no exports, no version confusion, no "that was last month's file".
